# Topical Authority — toilet.partisi.co.id

## Role and boundary

`toilet.partisi.co.id` is a Syamsul-owned knowledge and commercial subdomain for toilet cubicles, doors, urinal screens, panels, pilasters, and hardware in Indonesian public and commercial washrooms. It should help owners, architects, accessibility specialists, contractors, facility teams, cleaners, operators, and buyers specify and manage complete systems.

Existing material, configuration, application, and location routes own quote intent. Knowledge pages must not infer hygiene, water resistance, fire, impact, structural, privacy, or accessibility performance from a material name or marketing tier alone. Complete system evidence, room context, manufacturer data, current requirements, qualified design, and correct installation govern outcomes.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Verify current accessibility, fire, glazing, structural/anchor, chemical, public-health, occupational-safety, manufacturer, and local project requirements before publishing dimensions, forces, ratings, test claims, or compliance statements.
- Performance claims must identify test method, exact specimen/system, laboratory, date, classification, scope, and limitations.
- Use original survey drawings/photos, calibrated measurements, material samples, matched certificates, installation records, and qualified review.
- Never fabricate prices, projects, test data, hygiene outcomes, warranties, stock, or service coverage.
- Cleaning advice must match finish/hardware manufacturer guidance and chemical safety data; safety-critical defects require isolation.
